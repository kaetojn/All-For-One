# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

Installed as a home-screen PWA on iPhone (`display: standalone`, `viewport-fit=cover`). Mobile web, not native.

## Users

One person: the owner. The first sign-up claims the app and sign-up closes, and the owner has confirmed it is built for themself alone. Design for their habits, their gym and their kitchen, not for a general audience or for other self-hosters.

They use it in three situations:

- **Daily check-in at home:** logging weight, a food check (on target or not, calories, protein) and tape measurements, usually one-handed on the phone.
- **Mid-workout at the gym:** between sets, sweaty hands, quick glances. Tap to complete an exercise, adjust sets, reps and weight, check the start/end photos.
- **Kitchen and grocery store:** reading the weekly meal plan and the shopping list.

## Product Purpose

A personal diet and fitness tracker with Claude as the coach. The app does the logging and shows the results; Claude, through the connector, reads the data, reviews it, and adjusts the workout plan, calorie target and meal plan, leaving notes the owner reads in the app.

Success in a typical week:

1. **Daily check-ins stick.** Opening the app every day is effortless and habitual.
2. **Planned workouts get done,** with progression from session to session.

## Positioning

A single-user tracker whose coach is the owner's own Claude. The app is the instrument panel and logbook; the thinking happens in Claude via the MCP connector at `/mcp`, and every change Claude makes arrives with a note the owner can read. No generic coaching engine, no social layer, no other users.

## Operating Context

- Mostly an iPhone home-screen app; desktop is not a regular context.
- Workouts: a plan of ordered days built from the owner's equipment and target areas. One tap completes an exercise; values pre-fill from last time. After a workout: effort 1–10, a feeling, optional symptoms.
- First run: five questions (goal, level, time, equipment, basic numbers) builds a starter plan.
- Gradual unlocks: trends, body measurements and fine-tuning open after 3, 7 and 14 check-in days; a switch in More opens everything.
- Claude reviews run as a recurring task through the connector and post a summary to the home screen.
- Apple Health data arrives through an iPhone Shortcut posting to `/api/health/ingest`.
- Reminders: at most two a day, sent only when there is something to do.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack:** one Cloudflare Worker serves the static web app (`public/`, no build step, no dependencies), the API, OAuth and the MCP connector. D1 database, R2 for photos. Node 22+ for tests and the local preview (`node scripts/dev-server.js`, port 8787).
- **Tracking:** weight, tape measurements, daily food check, body fat by the US Navy method, goal and calorie target. An aggressive pace gets a warning and a safer alternative; the owner chooses.
- **3D body:** rotatable model shaped by measurements, with a projected goal shape and a skin-tone choice.
- **Photos:** private progress and meal photos; instant estimates only when `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set.
- **Food:** store, budget, household and dietary needs; weekly meal plan and shopping list written by Claude; a price list Claude fills in (starts empty).
- **Health safety:** conditions and post-workout symptoms are recorded; warning signs produce a clear stop message (`alert`) distinct from ordinary advice (`notice`). This must stay unmistakable.
- **Units:** metric or imperial, chosen at first run.
- **Exercise library:** generated by `scripts/build-exercises.py` from Free Exercise DB.
- **Not done:** the recurring review task in Claude is created once the connector works; the grocery price list fills on first refresh.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** All For One.
- **The bear cub:** the companion, a small cartoon bear in a sweatband (drawn in 3D from simple shapes in `public/mascot.js`). It has a deliberately small role: it appears only for moments worth marking (finishing a workout, a workout already done today, streak milestones), never on data, and not when a stop warning is showing. It replaced the earlier flame mascot, Ember.
- **Voice:** plain, short, encouraging without hype ("Five quick questions and you're in.", "No wrong answers here."). Says what an effort level means; states warnings directly.

## Product Principles

1. **The daily check-in comes first.** Anything that makes opening, logging and closing slower works against the main success measure.
2. **Usable mid-set.** Gym screens must work at a glance and with an imprecise tap.
3. **Claude decides, the app shows why.** Every plan or target change carries a readable note; the app never changes things silently.
4. **Safety outranks motivation.** Pace warnings and stop messages are never softened or hidden behind encouragement.
5. **Reveal gradually.** New depth unlocks with consistency rather than front-loading every feature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal standard set. Existing practice to keep: ARIA labels and pressed states on all controls, live region for screen updates, light and dark themes, large tap targets for gym use.
